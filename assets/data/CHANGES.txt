Fantasma website data: what changed from rc Water 11.12 to rc Grass 11.57
==========================================================================

The public release is Fantasma Part I, rc Grass, build 11.65 (tag rc-grass, first published 23 November 2026 as build 11.57 and updated 24 November 2026). None of this data changed between 11.57 and 11.65.
Fourteen rows changed since rc Water. No species' stats, types, abilities, Sigils or evolutions changed.

Wild encounters (4 changes, all in Lunar Woods, grass)

  - TEDDIURSA drops from 40% to 20%, by day and at night (two changed rows).

Gifts, statics and trades (4 new rows)

    until you turn on Show spoilers.

Don Dex, "where to find" only (6 species)
  - Shroomish: Lunar Woods added.
  - Breloom: its family is now first found in Lunar Woods (was Petalburg Woods).
