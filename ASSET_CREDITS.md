# Third-Party Asset Credits

Fantasma uses free-to-use community assets. This file credits every external
asset in the game, per the asset authors' licensing wishes.

## Source: Team Aqua's Asset Repo
A community library of free-to-use Gen-3 decomp assets.
<https://github.com/TeamAquasHideout/Team-Aquas-Asset-Repo>

License posture: assets are free to use and edit by default; a few are marked
"not free to edit" in their folder README and we respect that. Credit to the
original creator is required for every asset used.

### Battle backgrounds
Every battle background in the game is an original Fantasma painting, made with
Gemini at the owner's direction. No community battle-background art ships, so
none is credited.

### Character sprites (in use)
- **spilledpizza** — DP_Scientist overworld sprite, the shared base for the
  Kanto color-coat lab aides.
- **hyo** (Steven and Wally overworld redraws), via the GBA Sprites Pack.

### Hoenn overworld tiles (in use)
- **Game Freak / Nintendo** — Pokémon Diamond/Pearl/Platinum long grass,
  via **Bulbapedia**. Fantasma maps its shading to moonlit greens, joins and
  varies its blades, and bakes the original tips/roots into ragged cell
  borders. It replaced the earlier Minish Quest grass, none of which ships any
  more.
- **Roger Wrightshoe (aka Gray Ninja)** — plain ground art from
  `Tilesets/Other Tilesets/Roger Wrightshoe/greenery.png` in **Team Aqua's Asset
  Repo**. Fantasma quantizes and recolors the ground for its moonlit palette.
  Unbound's park was a ground color reference; its pixels are not imported.
  Source credit request: the folder's `README.md`.
- **LeoB (leob0505)** — ORAS-style Hoenn General terrain, Petalburg town
  tiles and matching door animations, from **Team Aqua's Asset Repo**, folder
  `Tilesets/The Great Tileset Exchange/Full Tilesets/LeoB ORAS/`.
  Inspired by **TheDeadHeroAlistair** and **Pokemon Omega Ruby/Alpha Sapphire**,
  as credited in the author's `credits.md`. Fantasma preserves gameplay
  attributes and merges its custom town dressing and legacy-town palettes.

### Tiles in the ROM, not used by any map
- **Princess-phoenix** and the **Pokémon Rejuvenation team** — the Pyramid
  Interior tileset, in the insertable version by **Rahtak**, from **Team Aqua's
  Asset Repo** (The Great Tileset Exchange). Credit required. It was brought in
  while the Golden Mine was being designed and is compiled into the game, but
  no map uses it. The Battle Pyramid's own tiles are Game Freak's.

**spilledpizza**, with **TheWiggliestJiggliest**, **RichardPT**, **robloxmaster376**,
**The Spriters Resource** and the **Radiant Quartz / Prismatic Platinum team**

## Source: Unbound-style start menu
The START and Tools menu icons are **Fantasma originals, painted with Gemini at
the owner's direction**, sampled to a shared 16-colour GBA palette.
**miriamlefae** and the USM branch authors (framework) are credited; see
`CREDITS.md`.

## Lorenzo's Ledger statues

The five League trophies (Aggron, Goodra, Duraludon, Zoroark and Kingambit)
are Fantasma originals painted with Gemini at the owner's direction.
The card uses the HQ trophy stone palette; Kingambit's plinth keeps its
tarnished gold band.

The five Ledger page paintings (gym cabinet, League altar, god reliquary,
sealed page and ledger front) are also owner-commissioned Gemini art.

## Credits-roll backgrounds and battle boards

The five paintings behind the end-credits text and the Hall of Fame backdrop
are owner-commissioned Gemini art. Not attributed to a third party.

Every battle board in the game, including the Sky Pillar tower interior, the
Fantasma Zone, the drowned Kyogre sea temple, the stadium and its six palette
tints, the location boards and the Golden Mine boards, is an owner-commissioned
Gemini painting in Fantasma's violet-teal style. No third-party art was reused.
The owner ruled the paintings moody enough to need no night versions, so the
tall grass and sand night boards are copies of the day boards.

## Music

Custom Fantasma music and sound (the Fantasma motif and the boot bell) is
original to this project and was made for Fantasma with AI assistance, with no
third-party source. All other music is the original Pokémon Emerald soundtrack
(Game Freak / Nintendo) carried by pokeemerald-expansion.

## Golden Mine tiles

The Golden Mine's deep tiles and its coolant plant pieces (gate pipe, valve
housing, ice bank, pipe junction) were cut from owner-commissioned Gemini
(Google) sheets, as was the pushable crate sprite. The mine's floor is Granite
Cave's own floor from Pokémon Emerald, a Game Freak asset already in the base
game; no new credit.
