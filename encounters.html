Part I · rc Grass 11.65

Wild Encounters

One table for each area, method and time of day in Hoenn. 119 areas, 3,305 rows, checked against the 11.57 ROM and unchanged in 11.65. Kanto, the Sevii Islands, Battle Frontier facilities and Remix mode are not included.

Levels. The numbers here are the table levels — the most a fully progressed player sees. In play, the game lowers wild levels so they stay 2 under the player’s current level cap (3 under in the Safari Zone).

Rates. The rate number on each table is the area’s raw encounter rate, not a percent. A species’ chance is its share of that method in that area, and each table adds up to 100%.

Places the in-game Dex hides until found stay hidden until you turn this on.
