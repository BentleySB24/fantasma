The story

Premise

You play Lorenzo della Pira, a ghost known as Il Fantasma. Hoenn does not need a hero from you. It will learn to fear you.

You begin as a bare ghost. Defeat trainers and take their abilities as Sigils, then stitch those Sigils onto any Pokémon in your party. Steal the faces of Gym Leaders and the Don, and walk their towns as someone else. People answer the identity standing in front of them. Word of the Fantasma spreads, the heat rises, and hunters come to bring you in.

Part I is the full Hoenn story: the League, the post-League God Hunt, and its own ending. Finish it and Unfair and Nuzlocke unlock for your next run. Part II, set in Kanto, is in development.

What you can do

Features

Built on pokeemerald-expansion.

Sigils — steal any ability

More than 100 Sigil items, each a stolen ability, used like a nature mint. Put Huge Power on a Slaking or Volt Absorb on a Gyarados. Enemies fight fire with fire, and some wear custom abilities of their own.

Disguises — wear their faces

Eight or more disguises. Play as the ghost, or steal identities as you move through the gym ranks. Townspeople, trainers and fallen Gym members respond to the face you wear.

A wanted system

The more evil you do, the more the heat rises, and hunters come to ambush you. Think of it as a wanted level: Hoenn does not just watch. It hunts.

Original maps and quests

Handmade in Porymap: Lunar Woods, Bellgrave Abbey and the Cellar HQ. Rob the mart, crack the Game Corner vault, hunt down Hoenn’s gods after the League, and a run of side stories.

Golden Grave

An in-game slot machine that pays out items, mints and some special Sigils you cannot get anywhere else. The hotter your heat, the better the odds.

Fifty-plus boss battles

Gym Leaders, the League, hunters, and the gods of the post-game hunt. Vanilla difficulty uses level caps. The in-game Guide can scout a boss before a required fight.

Little grind, a Memory Altar

Cap Candy, level catch-up, EV Ashes and IV Salts (like bottle caps). The Memory Altar teaches level-up, TM, egg and move tutor moves in one place.

Tools and quality of life

A custom menu with Venom Cup (pre-poison your party), Grave Dial (edit the time) and Spirit Sight (reveals hidden Pokémon and secrets). Life Flask party heals, improved Fly, sneak, a portable PC, Phantom Charm (a repel or lure that never runs out), speed controls, Litwick Candle (auto-Flash), a Type Chart item, move relearning anywhere, and boss scouting.

Get it running

How to patch

Recommended: use the Hackdex page and follow the steps there. Your ROM stays in your browser. If you would rather patch by hand, use the steps below. This site never gives you a ROM.

  1. Download Fantasma Part I rc Water.zip from the latest GitHub release, or patch through Hackdex.
  2. Obtain a clean Pokémon Emerald (USA/Europe) ROM dumped from your own cartridge. It should be 16,777,216 bytes, with CRC32 1F1C08FB.
  3. Apply the .bps patch with Rom Patcher JS, Floating IPS, or any BPS patcher.
  4. Confirm the patched game is 33,554,432 bytes, CRC32 86E56261.
  5. Play on mGBA (tested). If your emulator asks for a save type, choose Flash 128K.
  6. Start a NEW GAME. Save with the in-game SAVE option and keep that save for later versions. If you get stuck, START > GUIDE has a recap and an UNSTUCK option.

Questions

FAQ

What is Fantasma?

A free fan-made ROM hack of Pokémon Emerald by Don Duskull. You play Lorenzo della Pira, Il Fantasma: a ghost who steals abilities and identities and takes on a darker Hoenn. Part I, rc Water (build 11.12), was released on 15 November 2026.

Do you distribute ROM files?

No. Official downloads are a patch only: Hackdex is the main page, and GitHub Releases is the backup, with patch notes. You must supply a clean Pokémon Emerald (USA/Europe) ROM dumped from your own cartridge.

What patch format should I use?

BPS. The GitHub release is a zip that contains a .bps file. Apply it with Rom Patcher JS or another BPS patcher, or use the built-in patcher on Hackdex.

Is Part I finished? What about Kanto?

Part I is the complete Hoenn story, including the League, the God Hunt and a Part I ending. After the ending you can Continue and keep exploring Hoenn. Part II (Kanto) is in active development. Keeping Part I saves working in later versions is a goal, not a guarantee for Part II.

Which emulator should I use?

mGBA is the tested emulator. If you are asked for a save type, choose Flash 128K. Start a new game, and save with the in-game menu even if you also use emulator save states.

Was this made with AI tools?

Yes. Fantasma is a one-person project by Don Duskull, built with AI-assisted tools. The tools wrote the scripts and most of the code, drafted dialogue that was directed and edited, and painted battle backgrounds, menus and other new art. The concept, design, story direction, custom maps and playtesting are Don Duskull’s.

Where do I report a bug or leave playtest notes?

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Include the version (rc Water), your emulator and device, where you were and what you were doing, and a screenshot if you can. Playtest feedback is welcome even when nothing is broken.
